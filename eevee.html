Name/nickname characters (traditionally all-caps):
  • A-Z, a-z, spaces
  • - ? ! / . , ( ) : [ ] ; *
  • @ # (shown as PK MN)
Moveset notes:
  • No duplicate moves.
  • Empty slot(s) must be at the end.
  • Lettered moves can only be mixed as a+b, b+c, or c+d.
  • Moves with * are only valid at level 52+.
